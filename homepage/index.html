About

I am a University Lecturer and Docent (Adjunct Professor) in Computer Science and Engineering at the Department of Information Technologies, Åbo Akademi University in Turku, Finland. My docentship, awarded in 2024, is in Computational Modelling and Industrial Applications, and I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the same department in 2015.

My work bridges rigorous formal reasoning and applied artificial intelligence. Early in my career I developed formal methods for the quantitative refinement of reaction-based biomodels; today I apply machine learning and computer vision to real-world domains such as maritime navigation, remote sensing, and medical imaging — and I am returning to formal foundations through work on certifying model merging via refinement calculus.

Alongside research, I coordinate and teach in international master's programmes, supervise doctoral, master's, and bachelor's students, and contribute to the wider academic community as a reviewer, committee member, and advocate for equity in computing.
